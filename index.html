01 /PROFILE

From RTL to validated hardware.

I am an FPGA Engineer with hands-on experience in Xilinx digital systems, VHDL RTL, SystemVerilog verification and custom/vendor IP integration. I currently work in the defence industry on high-speed video interfaces and image acquisition and processing architectures.

I enjoy the full path: requirements and ICDs, microarchitecture, testbenches, synthesis, timing closure, and debug with lab instruments. My background also covers digital power control, embedded systems and hardware acceleration.
